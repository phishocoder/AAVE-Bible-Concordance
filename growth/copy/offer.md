# Offer Definition (draft)

## One-liner
AAVE Bible Concordance helps people understand Scripture fast—*in the language they actually speak*—so the Word hits real life, not just church life.

## Who it’s for
- People who grew up hearing Scripture but want it to feel *alive* and *plain*
- New believers who get stuck on “church words”
- Folks sharing the Gospel with friends/family who don’t talk “religious”
- Youth groups / small groups / men’s & women’s circles

## Primary promise
Make the Bible easier to understand and talk about—without watering it down.

## What makes it different
- Language that feels familiar and human
- Fast lookups (words, themes, references)
- Built for everyday reading + sharing

## Proof targets (to add)
- Testimonials (“I finally get what this means…”) 
- Before/after examples (verse → clearer explanation)
- Usage stats (time saved / sessions)

## CTAs
- “Download and try it today”
- “Send a verse to a friend”
- “Support the project” (donate)
