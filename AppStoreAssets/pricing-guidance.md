# Pricing Guidance

App: **AAVE Bible Concordance**

## Recommendation
- **Price:** Free

## Why
- There is no evidence in this repo of an existing paid tier, subscription system, or in-app purchase flow that should drive a paid App Store price for Version 1.0.
- A free launch lowers friction for:
  - churches
  - first-time readers
  - testers
  - community growth
  - feedback collection during early product shaping

## Product rationale
For Version 1.0, distribution and adoption matter more than direct paid conversion:
- easier to share
- easier to test with ministries and small groups
- easier to build leaderboard and reading habit momentum
- easier to collect real-world product feedback

## Manual action in App Store Connect
In `Pricing and Availability`, choose:
- **Price:** `Free`

## Revisit later if needed
You can revisit pricing later if you add:
- subscriptions
- premium commentary packs
- paid study plans
- church/group features

For this current repo state, **Free** is the cleanest and lowest-risk choice.
