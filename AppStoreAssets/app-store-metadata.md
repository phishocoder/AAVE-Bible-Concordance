## Description
AAVE Bible Concordance helps readers engage Scripture with clarity, culture, and context.

Explore Bible passages in an accessible AAVE-style reading experience, paired with commentary designed to make the text easier to understand and apply. Whether you are studying alone, preparing for Bible study, or just trying to reconnect with Scripture in a familiar voice, this app gives you a fresh way to engage the Word.

Features include:
• AAVE-style Bible reading experience
• Clear commentary for selected chapters
• Daily verse engagement
• Bible quizzes to test your understanding
• Bookmarks for saving key passages
• Share and copy tools for study and reflection
• Leaderboards and community learning features

This app is built as a cultural and educational Bible study tool, helping more people understand Scripture without feeling locked out by confusing language.

AAVE Bible Concordance is not trying to replace traditional Bible translations. It is a companion experience designed to help readers connect with Scripture through clarity, context, and cultural familiarity.
