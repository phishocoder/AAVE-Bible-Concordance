# App Store Category Guidance

App: **AAVE Bible Concordance**

## Recommendation
- **Primary Category:** Reference
- **Secondary Category:** Education

Optional alternate secondary:
- **Books**

## Why `Reference` should be primary
This app is fundamentally a Bible reference and study product:
- Scripture reading
- Verse lookup and navigation
- Commentary access
- Translation comparison
- Bookmarks and notes
- Study-oriented search and reflection flows

The core value is helping readers **reference, interpret, and revisit Scripture**.

## Why `Education` works well as secondary
The app also includes clear learning mechanics:
- Quizzes
- Reading streaks
- Commentary for understanding
- Study progression and habit-building

That makes `Education` a strong secondary category if you want to emphasize learning and engagement.

## Why `Books` is the alternate
`Books` is reasonable if you want to position the app more like a reading library than a study platform. I would still rank it behind `Education` because the app is more interactive than a straightforward reading-only books app.

## Final recommendation to enter manually
- Primary: `Reference`
- Secondary: `Education`
