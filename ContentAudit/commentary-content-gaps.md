# Commentary Content Gaps

These references intentionally have no bundled commentary because the previous
records were exact copies of the AAVE scripture text. They should remain
unavailable in the app until distinct, editorially reviewed commentary is
provided.

- Leviticus 1:1–17
- 1 Corinthians 3:23
- 1 Corinthians 13:6

The July 23, 2026 audit found 11,935 original commentary records and removed
these 19 exact scripture copies. All 66 AAVE book files and all 66 commentary
files now parse as valid JSON, and no other exact duplicates were found where a
same-reference AAVE verse exists.

The audit also found 60 older commentary records whose chapter/verse reference
does not exist in the corresponding AAVE book. They are not reachable from a
valid reader verse and should be reviewed as a separate content-cleanup pass.
