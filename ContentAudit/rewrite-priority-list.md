## Ranking Method

- Tier order follows the requested priority: `SOLEMN`, `PROPHECY`, `TEACHING`, `POETIC`, then `DIALOGUE/NARRATIVE` last. `WISDOM` is included after poetic and before story/dialogue because it carries instructional weight.

- Slang density combines flagged term counts from verse text with high-priority rows from `aave-tone-audit.md`.
- Sacred weight increases for divine speech, judgment/covenant language, God/Jesus/Spirit references, sin, wrath, blessing/curse, temple, blood, cross, glory, grace, and truth.
